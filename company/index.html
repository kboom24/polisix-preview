Polisix / The Polisix Standard

Power is nothing without principles.

Polisix is the business-to-business side of an insurance customer-acquisition company. We acquire customers under our own consumer brands and deliver them, consented and verified, to licensed professionals. Here is the standard we hold ourselves to.

The Polisix Standard

Six commitments. No exceptions.

They bind every lead, call, course and campaign we deliver, written for the next hundred years, not the next quarter.

01

Consent before contact

No consumer is contacted without documented, seller-specific consent we can produce on request.

02

Quality over volume

We would rather deliver fewer customers than the wrong ones. Returns are honored by policy.

03

One standard for all

The single agent and the national carrier get the same verification, records and respect.

04

Transparent pricing

Clear definitions of a billable lead or call, and invoices that reconcile to the record.

05

Licensed and accountable

Customers only reach agents licensed where they live. We stand behind every handoff.

06

Built to last

We invest in people, training and infrastructure that outlast market cycles and serve generations of agents.

Built for the next century.

Our standards are public, our records are auditable and our partnerships are designed to run for decades.
